The Elements of Love

Astrological compatibility relies heavily on how the four primary elements interact with one another:

  • Fire (Aries, Leo, Sag): Brings passion, high energy, and spontaneous adventure.
  • Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Cap): Offers stability, practical support, and long-term security.
  • Air (Gemini, Libra, Aqu): Drives communication, intellectual spark, and social bonds.
  • Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): Enhances emotional depth, intuition, and deep empathy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kundali?

A Kundli (also known as a Janam Kundali, birth chart, or natal chart) is a symbolic map of the cosmos at the exact time and place of a person's birth. Rooted in Vedic astrology (Jyotish Shastra), it is used to understand personality, navigate life events, and check compatibility for marriage.

How Horoscope Works?

Horoscopes operate by interpreting the positions of the Sun, Moon, and planets as they move through the 12 zodiac signs and astrological houses

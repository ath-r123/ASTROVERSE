How Kundali Matching Works

Vedic astrology uses the Ashtakoota Milan system to analyze 8 different parameters (Kootas) based on the positions of the Moon in both charts:

  • Varna (1 Point): Work and spiritual compatibility.
  • Vashya (2 Points): Mutual attraction and dominance.
  • Tara (3 Points): Health, longevity, and well-being.
  • Yoni (4 Points): Physical intimacy and biological compatibility.
  • Grah Maitri (5 Points): Psychological harmony and friendship.
  • Gana (6 Points): Temperament and behavioral alignment.
  • Bhakoot (7 Points): Emotional connection and family growth.
  • Nadi (8 Points): Health of future children and genetic match.

Understanding the Score (Out of 36)

Above 18: Acceptable and stable match.

Above 25: Highly recommended, strong harmony.

Below 18: Not ideal; requires remedy options.

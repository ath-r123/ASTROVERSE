What is a Mulank?

In Vedic Numerology (Ank Jyotish), your Mulank is the single-digit sum of your exact birth date. It rules your basic behavior patterns and initial reactions to life:

  • The Calculation: If you are born on the 24th, your Mulank is 2 + 4 = 6.
  • The Core Identity: Unlike your Destiny Number (Bhagyank), Mulank represents your internal self and innate desires.
  • Planetary Alignment: Every Mulank from 1 to 9 is strictly governed by a specific celestial planet or deity.

✨ Quick Guide (1 to 9)

1: Sun (Leader) | 2: Moon (Creative) | 3: Jupiter (Guru) | 4: Rahu (Rebel) | 5: Mercury (Trader) | 6: Venus (Artist) | 7: Ketu (Mystic) | 8: Saturn (Judge) | 9: Mars (Warrior).
